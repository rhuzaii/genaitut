THE MODEL CAMPUS CONDUCT CODE, 2026
A FICTIONAL STATUTE WRITTEN SOLELY TO TEST THIS SOFTWARE. NOT LAW.

CHAPTER I OF PRELIMINARY MATTERS

1. Short title and extent.—This Code may be called the Model Campus Conduct Code, 2026. It extends to all premises owned or leased by the Institute and to all conduct occurring during an activity organised by the Institute, wherever that activity takes place.

2. Commencement.—This Code shall come into force on the first day of the academic session following its publication in the Institute Gazette.

3. Definitions.—In this Code, unless the context otherwise requires, "student" means a person registered for any programme of the Institute; "staff" means a person employed by the Institute in any capacity; "premises" includes hostels, laboratories, libraries and grounds; and "Committee" means the Disciplinary Committee constituted under section 20.

CHAPTER II OF GENERAL EXCEPTIONS

4. Act done under compulsion.—Nothing is an offence under this Code which is done by a person who is compelled to do it by threat of immediate harm to that person or to another, provided that the person did not voluntarily place themselves in the situation giving rise to the compulsion.

5. Act done in good faith for the benefit of another.—Nothing is an offence under this Code which is done in good faith for the benefit of another person, even if it causes that person inconvenience, provided the act was reasonable in the circumstances and no consent could be obtained in time.

6. Trivial acts.—Nothing is an offence under this Code by reason of any harm which it causes, if that harm is so slight that no person of ordinary sense and temper would complain of it.

CHAPTER III OF OFFENCES AGAINST ACADEMIC INTEGRITY

10. Plagiarism.—Whoever submits for assessment any work which reproduces the work of another person without attribution, intending that it be assessed as that person's own, commits plagiarism and shall be punished with reassessment of the work at zero marks, and, in a repeated instance, with suspension for one academic term.

11. Fabrication of results.—Whoever, in any report, dissertation or laboratory record submitted for assessment, states a result that was not obtained by the procedure described, commits fabrication of results and shall be punished with failure in the relevant course, and, where the fabrication is extensive, with suspension for one academic year.

12. Impersonation in an examination.—Whoever sits an examination in the place of another, and whoever procures another to sit an examination in their place, each commits impersonation and shall be punished with expulsion.

13. Unauthorised collaboration.—Whoever collaborates with another person on work that was required to be performed individually commits unauthorised collaboration and shall be punished with reduction of marks, provided that the Committee may excuse the act where the requirement was not clearly communicated.

14. Possession of prohibited material in an examination hall.—Whoever is found in an examination hall in possession of any note, device or material not expressly permitted by the examination notice shall be punished with cancellation of that examination paper, whether or not the material was used.

CHAPTER IV OF OFFENCES AGAINST PERSONS AND PROPERTY

20. Constitution of the Disciplinary Committee.—The Institute shall constitute a Disciplinary Committee consisting of three members of staff and two students, of whom at least one member shall be a woman, to hear and decide every proceeding under this Code.

21. Ragging.—Whoever does any act which causes, or is likely to cause, physical or psychological harm, apprehension or shame to a junior student, commits ragging and shall be punished with expulsion, and the punishment under this section shall not be reduced on the ground that the junior student consented.

22. Damage to Institute property.—Whoever causes damage to any property of the Institute shall be punished with the cost of restoration together with a fine not exceeding twice that cost, and, where the damage was caused wilfully, with suspension for one academic term in addition.

23. Obstruction of staff in the discharge of duty.—Whoever voluntarily obstructs any member of staff in the discharge of a duty under this Code shall be punished with suspension for a period not exceeding one academic term.

24. Unauthorised entry into a restricted area.—Whoever enters any laboratory, server room or storage area marked as restricted, without the permission of the officer in charge, shall be punished with a written warning, and, where the entry caused risk to safety, with suspension for one academic term.

CHAPTER V OF PROCEDURE

30. Notice of proceedings.—No person shall be punished under this Code unless that person has been given written notice of the allegation and a period of not less than seven days to respond.

31. Right to be heard.—Every person against whom a proceeding is brought shall be entitled to appear before the Committee, to be accompanied by one person of their choice, and to examine the material relied upon.

32. Appeal.—Any person aggrieved by a decision of the Committee may appeal to the Director within thirty days of the decision, and the Director may confirm, reduce or set aside the punishment but shall not increase it.

33. Limitation.—No proceeding shall be commenced under this Code more than one year after the date on which the alleged conduct came to the notice of the Institute.
